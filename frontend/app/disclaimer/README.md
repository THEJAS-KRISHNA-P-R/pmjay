# `/disclaimer` — Legal & Medical Disclaimer

## Purpose
Provides explicit, uncompromised liability disclaimers for PMJAY Advocate, clarifying that this software is an educational/informational verification aid and does not constitute formal legal representation, clinical medical advice, or an advocate-client relationship.

## Key Disclaimers Documented
1. **Not Medical Advice**: Clinical treatment decisions remain with treating medical personnel.
2. **Not Legal Representation**: Automated scripts are not formal legal pleadings; users are directed to NALSA (15100) for free legal counsel.
3. **Emergency Care Priority**: Explicit instruction that emergency medical care must always precede financial disputes.
4. **Data Accuracy**: Explanation of NHA HBP package schedules and state-specific variations.
