# `/terms` — Terms of Service

## Purpose
Establishes the terms of use, limitation of liability, non-agency statement, and emergency medical priority conditions for users accessing PMJAY Advocate.

## Key Legal Provisions
1. **Mandatory Care-First Clause**: Users are explicitly warned never to delay urgent or emergency medical treatment over billing disputes.
2. **Non-Agency Statement**: Explicit disclaimer of government affiliation (not NHA, MoHFW, or SHA).
3. **Limitation of Liability**: As-is data provision with zero liability for healthcare provider decisions or package inaccuracies.
4. **Official Grievance Routing**: Clear pathways to CGRMS, NHA 14555, and NALSA 15100.
